Inspirational Reflection

Ministering to God: The First Calling of the Church

Before the church ministers to people, it is first called to minister to God. In Acts 13:2, the believers at Antioch were worshipping the Lord and fasting when the Holy Spirit spoke and set apart Barnabas and Saul for His work. Direction came to those who were first devoted to God Himself.

To minister to God is to give Him our attention, our worship and our obedience for His own sake, and not only for what we hope to receive. Mary of Bethany chose to sit at the feet of Jesus (Luke 10:39), and her devotion was honoured. When the singers and musicians in Solomon's temple praised the Lord with one voice, the glory of God filled the house (2 Chronicles 5:13–14). Power and presence follow adoration.

The same calling is open to every believer. Three simple practices keep a life centred on God:

Wait on Him daily. Set apart time each day for prayer and the Word, and let His voice order your day.
Worship in spirit and truth. As Jesus taught in John 4:23–24, the Father seeks worshippers who honour Him sincerely.
Obey promptly. Love for God is shown in quick, joyful obedience to what He has spoken.

We invite you to minister to God with us at our weekly prayer meetings, and to experience for yourself the truth of our theme: the effective, fervent prayer of a righteous man avails much (James 5:16).

Prayer Embassy Ministries

About Prayer Embassy Ministries (PEM)

Prayer Embassy Ministries, located in the Mutundwe and Kisigula neighbourhood of Kampala, is a vibrant community of faith dedicated to spiritual transformation, fervent prayer and community impact.

Core Annual & Monthly Programmes

Ministers' Conferences

Special gatherings designed to equip, empower and unite church leaders and believers for spiritual growth and effective ministry.

Monthly Prayer & Fasting

A dedicated five-day period of fasting and prayer held at the end of every month to seek God's direction and breakthrough.

Charity Services & Christmas Giving

Community outreach programmes that support the needy and sharing love during the festive season to uplift vulnerable members of society.

Evangelism & Outreach

Dynamic local and cross-border outreach initiatives that take the gospel message from nation to nation.
